Data controller

Aldo Gonzalez Carlos, a software developer based in Mexico, is responsible for the personal data collected through the systems and WhatsApp numbers I manage. Contact: aldogcarlos@gmail.com.

Data collected

When someone uses one of these services through WhatsApp or a web console, the following may be collected:

  • Name and phone number.
  • Messages the person sends, including text, photos and shared location.
  • Information the person provides for their request, depending on the service (for example, vehicle, order or appointment details).
  • Date and time of each message and of each step of the request.

No financial, health or other sensitive personal data is collected.

How it is used

  • To receive and handle the requests the person sends.
  • To update the person, and those involved in handling the request, on its progress.
  • To keep a record of requests for follow-up and to improve the service.

The data is not used for advertising and is never sold.

Who it is shared with

Data is shared only with the people involved in handling the request (for example, the client company providing the service to the person) and with providers that make the service possible: WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) for message delivery and the system's hosting provider. These providers process it only to deliver their service.

How long it is kept

Data is kept while the person uses the service and for up to 12 months after their last request, unless they ask for it to be deleted sooner.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, correct it, have it deleted or object to its use (ARCO rights under Mexican law), and you can withdraw your consent. Email aldogcarlos@gmail.com with your name, your phone number and your request. I will reply within 20 business days.

How to request deletion of your data

Email aldogcarlos@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the phone number you used with the service. I will delete your personal data and messages within 30 days and confirm by email when it's done. Only what the law requires is kept.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, I will publish the new version on this page with its update date.
