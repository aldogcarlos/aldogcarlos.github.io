What I do

I'm an independent software developer. I work with each client from the idea to launch: I learn how the business runs, propose the simplest solution that solves the problem, then build it, ship it and maintain it.

  • Custom web systems Admin dashboards, customer portals and internal tools that replace spreadsheets and manual processes, with users, permissions and reports.

  • Prototypes Working versions in a few weeks to test an idea with real users before investing in it.

How I use WhatsApp

The WhatsApp numbers I manage are used only for service messages the person asked for: confirming a request, updating them on its progress or sharing a support contact. I don't send advertising or bulk messages. You can read how I handle data in the privacy notice.
